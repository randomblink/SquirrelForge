# SquirrelForge Business Continuity

## Purpose

The Business Continuity component ensures that SquirrelForge continues delivering essential platform capabilities during operational disruptions, infrastructure failures, cyber incidents, degraded performance, or disaster recovery events.

Business Continuity focuses on maintaining critical services at acceptable service levels until full platform recovery has been achieved. It coordinates continuity strategies, degraded operating modes, service prioritization, operational communications, and continuity governance.

Business Continuity does not perform recovery operations. It works alongside the Recovery Manager and Disaster Recovery to preserve operational capability throughout the disruption.

---

# Responsibilities

- Maintain essential platform operations.
- Prioritize critical services.
- Coordinate degraded operating modes.
- Allocate limited resources.
- Preserve critical workflows.
- Coordinate continuity communications.
- Monitor continuity effectiveness.
- Record continuity activity.
- Support resilience governance.
- Enable safe transition back to normal operations.

---

# Inputs

The Business Continuity component receives:

- Disaster recovery status
- Recovery progress reports
- Service availability reports
- Infrastructure status
- Resource availability
- Business priorities
- Governance policies
- Security assessments
- Observability reports
- Platform state

---

# Outputs

The Business Continuity component produces:

- Continuity plans
- Service prioritization schedules
- Resource allocation recommendations
- Degraded mode activation requests
- Continuity status reports
- Operational communications
- Governance review requests
- Business continuity audit records

---

# Business Continuity Workflow

1. Receive continuity event.
2. Assess operational impact.
3. Identify critical platform functions.
4. Prioritize available resources.
5. Activate continuity strategies.
6. Monitor essential operations.
7. Adjust service priorities as needed.
8. Coordinate return to normal operations.
9. Record audit information.
10. Publish continuity status.

---

# Continuity Priorities

Essential operations are prioritized as follows:

1. Governance
2. Security
3. Identity and authentication
4. Core AI Driver services
5. Workflow execution
6. Memory and knowledge
7. Automation
8. Observability
9. Optimization
10. Non-critical services

# Resource Management

Resource allocation considers:

- Business priority
- User impact
- Platform stability
- Security requirements
- Recovery objectives
- Available capacity
- Operational risk
- Governance requirements

---

# Continuity Verification

The Business Continuity component verifies:

- Essential services remain available.
- Critical workflows continue.
- Security remains active.
- Governance remains enforced.
- Observability remains functional.
- Recovery objectives remain achievable.
- User impact is minimized.

---

# Return to Normal Operations

Before returning to full operation:

- Recovery must be verified.
- Platform stability confirmed.
- Security controls validated.
- Service integrity confirmed.
- Governance approval obtained.
- Resource utilization normalized.
- Monitoring confirms stable operation.

- Observability Layer
- Security Layer

- Resilience Governance

---

# Data Protection

The Business Continuity component must:

- Protect continuity plans.
- Preserve operational evidence.
- Protect business priorities.
- Enforce governance policies.
- Maintain audit integrity.

---

# Safety Rules

The Business Continuity component must never:

- Disable critical security controls.
- Ignore governance requirements.
- Sacrifice data integrity.
- Resume full operation without verification.
- Suppress continuity failures.
- Delete continuity records.
- Prioritize convenience over safety.

---

# Failure Handling

If business continuity fails:

- Preserve operational state.
- Record continuity failures.
- Notify the Resilience Manager.
- Escalate to Disaster Recovery when required.
- Trigger contingency procedures.
- Maintain audit continuity.
- Protect essential platform capabilities.

---

# Audit Requirements

Every business continuity operation records:

- Continuity operation ID
- Timestamp
- Operating mode
- Critical services maintained
- Resource allocation summary
- Governance status
- Verification status
- Final outcome

---

# Success Criteria

The Business Continuity component succeeds when:

- Essential platform operations continue.
- Critical services remain available.
- User impact is minimized.
- Resources are allocated effectively.
- Security and governance remain enforced.
- Safe transition back to normal operation occurs.
- Audit records remain complete.
