# SquirrelForge Resilience Governance

## Purpose

The Resilience Governance component establishes the policies, standards, controls, and oversight governing all resilience activities across SquirrelForge. It ensures that failure detection, recovery, rollback, self-healing, redundancy, failover, disaster recovery, and business continuity operate safely, consistently, transparently, and in compliance with platform governance requirements.

Resilience Governance does not perform recovery activities. It defines the operational boundaries, approval requirements, verification standards, and audit expectations that apply throughout the resilience lifecycle.

---

# Responsibilities

- Define resilience governance policies.
- Establish resilience standards.
- Govern resilience lifecycles.
- Approve resilience procedures.
- Enforce recovery verification.
- Manage resilience risks.
- Coordinate governance reviews.
- Record governance activity.
- Support continuous improvement.
- Maintain governance consistency.

# Governance Workflow

1. Receive governance request.
2. Identify applicable resilience policies.
3. Review supporting evidence.
4. Evaluate compliance requirements.
5. Assess operational and security risks.
6. Approve, defer, or reject governance actions.
7. Publish governance decision.
8. Record governance activity.
9. Notify affected resilience components.
10. Schedule governance review.

# Data Protection

Resilience Governance must:

- Protect governance records.
- Preserve resilience evidence.
- Protect recovery documentation.
- Enforce access controls.
- Maintain audit integrity.

---

# Safety Rules

Resilience Governance must never:

- Permit unauthorized recovery actions.
- Weaken security controls.
- Ignore verification requirements.
- Bypass governance procedures.
- Suppress resilience failures.
- Delete protected audit records.
- Restore services without validation.

---

# Failure Handling

If governance operations fail:

- Preserve governance evidence.
- Record governance failures.
- Notify the Resilience Manager.
- Escalate unresolved governance issues.
- Maintain existing protections.
- Continue audit recording.
- Schedule governance review.

---

# Audit Requirements

Every governance operation records:

- Governance operation ID
- Timestamp
- Resilience component
- Policy evaluated
- Governance decision
- Risk classification
- Reviewer
- Final outcome

---

# Success Criteria

Resilience Governance succeeds when:

- All resilience activities follow approved governance processes.
- Recovery operations remain safe and verifiable.
- Security and data integrity are preserved.
- Operational risks are proactively managed.
- Recovery actions remain observable and auditable.
- Governance policies remain current and effective.
- Platform resilience continues to improve through measured oversight.
