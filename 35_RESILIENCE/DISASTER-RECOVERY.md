# SquirrelForge Disaster Recovery

## Purpose

The Disaster Recovery component coordinates the restoration of SquirrelForge following catastrophic failures that exceed normal recovery capabilities. It provides structured procedures for recovering from major infrastructure failures, cyber incidents, widespread data loss, regional outages, and other large-scale operational disruptions.

Disaster Recovery does not replace normal recovery operations. It activates only when authorized resilience policies determine that standard recovery procedures are insufficient.

---

# Responsibilities

- Coordinate disaster recovery operations.
- Establish recovery priorities.
- Restore critical platform services.
- Coordinate infrastructure restoration.
- Verify platform integrity.
- Support business continuity.
- Coordinate stakeholder communications.
- Record disaster recovery activity.
- Support observability.
- Enforce resilience governance.

---

# Inputs

The Disaster Recovery component receives:

- Disaster declarations
- Catastrophic failure reports
- Infrastructure assessments
- Recovery policies
- Business continuity plans
- Recovery objectives
- Governance policies
- Security assessments
- Platform state
- Observability reports

---

# Outputs

The Disaster Recovery component produces:

- Disaster recovery plans
- Recovery execution requests
- Infrastructure restoration requests
- Priority restoration schedules
- Recovery progress reports
- Governance review requests
- Disaster recovery audit records

---

# Disaster Recovery Workflow

1. Receive disaster declaration.
2. Validate disaster classification.
3. Assess platform impact.
4. Activate disaster recovery procedures.
5. Prioritize critical services.
6. Coordinate infrastructure restoration.
7. Verify platform stability.
8. Restore normal operations.
9. Record audit information.
10. Publish disaster recovery status.

---

# Disaster Categories

Supported disaster scenarios include:

- Infrastructure loss
- Data center outage
- Cloud service failure
- Cybersecurity incident
- Data corruption
- Regional outage
- Critical dependency failure
- Power failure
- Network isolation
- Multi-system failure

# Recovery Priorities

Recovery priority follows:

1. Governance systems
2. Security systems
3. Identity and authentication
4. Core platform services
5. Data integrity
6. AI Driver
7. Workflow execution
8. Automation
9. Observability
10. Optimization and analytics

- Security verification
- Operational validation
- Business continuity alignment

# Recovery Phases

Disaster recovery progresses through:

- Declaration
- Assessment
- Stabilization
- Infrastructure recovery
- Service restoration
- Data verification
- Operational validation

# Verification Requirements

Before declaring recovery complete, verify:

- Core services operational
- Data integrity confirmed
- Security controls restored
- Authentication functioning
- AI systems operational
- Workflows executing correctly
- Observability functioning
- Governance compliance restored

---

# Data Protection

The Disaster Recovery component must:

- Protect recovery evidence.
- Preserve forensic information.
- Protect recovery plans.
- Enforce governance policies.
- Maintain audit integrity.

---

# Safety Rules

The Disaster Recovery component must never:

- Restore compromised systems without verification.
- Ignore data integrity concerns.
- Bypass governance requirements.
- Weaken security controls.
- Suppress disaster evidence.
- Delete recovery records.
- Resume operations before validation.

---

# Failure Handling

If disaster recovery fails:

- Preserve recovery state.
- Record recovery failures.
- Notify the Resilience Manager.
- Escalate to executive governance.
- Trigger contingency procedures.
- Maintain audit continuity.
- Protect remaining operational assets.

---

# Audit Requirements

Every disaster recovery operation records:

- Disaster recovery ID
- Timestamp
- Disaster classification
- Recovery phase
- Services restored
- Verification status
- Governance status
- Final outcome

---

# Success Criteria

The Disaster Recovery component succeeds when:

- Critical services are restored.
- Platform integrity is preserved.
- Data recovery objectives are achieved.
- Security controls are fully restored.
- Governance requirements are enforced.
- Business operations resume safely.
- Audit records remain complete.
