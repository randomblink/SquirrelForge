# SquirrelForge Permissions

Version: 1.0.0
Status: Stable
Owner: Security Maintainers
Depends On: Governance Policy
Used By: Agents and Action Dispatcher
Last Updated: 2026-07-01

Permissions follow least privilege and are scoped by actor, action, resource, and duration. Read, write, execute, network, secret, and external-side-effect capabilities are evaluated separately. Denial is the default.
