# SquirrelForge Communication Governance

## Purpose

The Communication Governance component establishes the policies, standards, controls, and oversight governing all communication throughout SquirrelForge. It ensures that messages, conversations, notifications, events, inter-agent communication, service messaging, and archived communications remain secure, reliable, interoperable, observable, and auditable.

Communication Governance does not transport messages or perform communication activities. It defines the rules, protocols, approval requirements, retention standards, and compliance requirements that every Communication Layer component must follow.

---

# Responsibilities

- Define communication governance policies.
- Establish messaging standards.
- Govern communication protocols.
- Define authorization requirements.
- Enforce interoperability standards.
- Govern retention requirements.
- Coordinate governance reviews.
- Record governance activity.
- Support continuous improvement.
- Maintain communication consistency.

# Governance Workflow

1. Receive governance request.
2. Identify applicable communication policies.
3. Review supporting evidence.
4. Evaluate compliance requirements.
5. Assess operational and security risks.
6. Approve, defer, or reject governance actions.
7. Publish governance decision.
8. Record governance activity.
9. Notify affected communication components.
10. Schedule governance review.

# Data Protection

Communication Governance must:

- Protect governance records.
- Preserve communication evidence.
- Protect confidential communications.
- Enforce access controls.
- Maintain audit integrity.

---

# Safety Rules

Communication Governance must never:

- Permit unauthorized communication.
- Weaken communication security.
- Ignore retention requirements.
- Bypass governance procedures.
- Suppress communication failures.
- Delete protected audit records.
- Reduce communication traceability.

---

# Failure Handling

If governance operations fail:

- Preserve governance evidence.
- Record governance failures.
- Notify the Communication Manager.
- Escalate unresolved governance issues.
- Maintain existing protections.
- Continue audit recording.
- Schedule governance review.

---

# Audit Requirements

Every governance operation records:

- Governance operation ID
- Timestamp
- Communication component
- Policy evaluated
- Governance decision
- Risk classification
- Reviewer
- Final outcome

---

# Success Criteria

Communication Governance succeeds when:

- All communication follows approved standards.
- Messages remain secure, reliable, and traceable.
- Authorization and retention policies are enforced.
- Interoperability is maintained across the platform.
- Governance requirements remain current and effective.
- Communication risks are proactively managed.
- Audit records remain complete.
