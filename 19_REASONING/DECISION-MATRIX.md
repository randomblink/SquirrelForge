Score feasible options from 1–5 for rule compliance, requirement fit, maintainability, security, performance, delivery risk, reversibility, and evidence. Mandatory-rule failure disqualifies an option regardless of total. Record weights, evidence, uncertainty, rejected options, and the final rationale.
