# SquirrelForge Explanation Generator

## Purpose

The Explanation Generator produces clear, structured, and traceable explanations for the AI Driver's decisions, plans, recommendations, tool selections, and execution outcomes. It enables transparency, user understanding, governance review, debugging, and auditability while protecting confidential information and internal implementation details.

The Explanation Generator does not make decisions or modify platform behavior. It translates decision records and execution evidence into explanations appropriate for the intended audience.

---

# Responsibilities

- Generate decision explanations.
- Explain planning choices.
- Describe tool selections.
- Summarize execution outcomes.
- Support governance reviews.
- Produce audit-friendly narratives.
- Tailor explanations to audiences.
- Protect confidential information.
- Record explanation activity.
- Maintain explanation consistency.

---

The Explanation Generator receives:

- Decision records

# Explanation Workflow

1. Receive explanation request.
2. Identify target audience.
3. Gather supporting evidence.
4. Select relevant decision history.
5. Generate structured explanation.
6. Remove confidential information.
7. Validate explanation accuracy.
8. Record audit information.
9. Deliver explanation.
10. Archive explanation metadata.

---

# Safety Rules

The Explanation Generator must never:

- Fabricate explanations.
- Reveal confidential information.
- Misrepresent decisions.
- Expose protected reasoning artifacts.
- Ignore governance restrictions.
- Modify historical decision records.
- Execute actions directly.

---

# Failure Handling

If explanation generation fails:

- Preserve supporting evidence.
- Record explanation failures.
- Notify the AI Driver.
- Escalate persistent issues.
- Return a minimal factual explanation when appropriate.
- Maintain audit continuity.
- Avoid generating unsupported statements.

---

- Governance status
- Final outcome

- Confidential information is protected.

- Governance requirements are enforced.
