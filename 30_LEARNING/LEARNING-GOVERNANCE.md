# SquirrelForge Learning Governance

## Purpose

The Learning Governance component serves as the final authority for approving, rejecting, or restricting proposed adaptations within the Learning Layer. It ensures that every learning outcome complies with SquirrelForge's policies, safety requirements, security standards, validation rules, and operational objectives before any behavioral change is permitted.

Learning Governance protects the integrity of the platform by ensuring that learning remains transparent, evidence-based, reversible, and fully auditable.

---

# Responsibilities

- Review optimization proposals.
- Verify supporting evidence.
- Confirm validation completion.
- Evaluate operational risks.
- Enforce governance policies.
- Approve or reject adaptations.
- Apply operational restrictions.
- Maintain governance records.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Preserve complete audit history.

---

# Governance Inputs

Learning Governance reviews:

- Optimization proposals
- Evaluation reports
- Pattern analysis
- Validation results
- Risk assessments
- Security reviews
- Rule evaluations
- Performance impact estimates
- Rollback plans
- Historical learning records

---

# Governance Workflow

1. Receive adaptation proposal.
2. Verify proposal completeness.
3. Review supporting evidence.
4. Confirm validation results.
5. Assess operational risk.
6. Verify rollback readiness.
7. Evaluate policy compliance.
8. Issue governance decision.
9. Record governance outcome.
10. Notify the Adaptation Manager.

---

# Decision Types

Learning Governance may issue:

- Approved
- Approved with Conditions
- Deferred
- Requires Additional Evidence
- Rejected
- Permanently Prohibited

Each decision must include documented justification.

---

# Governance Evaluation Criteria

Every proposal is evaluated for:

- Evidence quality
- Validation completeness
- Rule compliance
- Security impact
- Safety impact
- Performance impact
- Operational benefit
- Reversibility
- Traceability
- Alignment with platform objectives

---

# Policy Enforcement

Learning Governance ensures that:

- Evidence supports every proposal.
- Validation is complete.
- Risks are documented.
- Rollback procedures exist.
- Security requirements are satisfied.
- Historical records remain intact.
- Unauthorized learning is prevented.

---

# Safety Rules

Learning Governance must never:

- Approve unsupported adaptations.
- Ignore validation failures.
- Override security policies.
- Permit irreversible changes.
- Remove audit records.
- Allow undocumented behavioral changes.

---

# Failure Handling

If governance review fails:

- Preserve the proposal.
- Record the failure.
- Notify the Learning Monitor.
- Request additional evidence if appropriate.
- Escalate unresolved issues.
- Maintain audit continuity.

---

# Audit Requirements

Every governance decision records:

- Governance ID
- Timestamp
- Proposal ID
- Decision type
- Decision rationale
- Evidence reviewed
- Risk assessment
- Conditions applied
- Reviewer component

---

# Success Criteria

Learning Governance succeeds when:

- Every proposal receives a documented decision.
- Only validated adaptations are approved.
- Risks are properly assessed.
- Security and policy requirements are enforced.
- Decisions remain fully traceable.
- Audit history is complete.
- Unauthorized learning never reaches production behavior.
