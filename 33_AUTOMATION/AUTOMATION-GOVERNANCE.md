# SquirrelForge Automation Governance

## Purpose

The Automation Governance component establishes the policies, standards, approval processes, and oversight required to ensure that all automation within SquirrelForge operates safely, securely, transparently, and in compliance with platform governance requirements.

Automation Governance defines how automation is proposed, validated, approved, executed, monitored, reviewed, modified, and retired throughout its lifecycle. It ensures that automation supports platform objectives without compromising security, reliability, observability, or auditability.

Automation Governance does not execute automation. It governs the policies and controls that apply to every automated activity across the platform.

---

# Responsibilities

- Define automation governance policies.
- Establish automation standards.
- Govern automation lifecycles.
- Enforce approval requirements.
- Review automation effectiveness.
- Manage automation risks.
- Coordinate governance reviews.
- Record governance activity.
- Support continuous improvement.
- Maintain governance consistency.

# Governance Workflow

1. Receive governance request.
2. Identify applicable policies.
3. Review automation documentation.
4. Evaluate validation and approval status.
5. Assess operational and security risks.
6. Approve, defer, or reject governance request.
7. Publish governance decision.
8. Record governance activity.
9. Notify affected components.
10. Schedule governance review.

# Safety Rules

Automation Governance must never:

- Permit unapproved automation.
- Weaken security requirements.
- Bypass governance controls.
- Ignore validation failures.
- Delete protected audit records.
- Override mandatory compliance requirements.
- Reduce platform observability.

---

# Failure Handling

If governance operations fail:

- Preserve governance evidence.
- Record governance failures.
- Notify the Automation Manager.
- Escalate unresolved governance issues.
- Maintain existing protections.
- Continue audit recording.
- Schedule governance review.

---

# Audit Requirements

Every governance operation records:

- Governance operation ID
- Timestamp
- Automation identifier
- Policy evaluated
- Governance decision
- Risk classification
- Reviewer
- Final outcome

---

# Success Criteria

Automation Governance succeeds when:

- All automation follows approved governance processes.
- Validation and approval requirements are consistently enforced.
- Security and compliance remain uncompromised.
- Automation remains observable and auditable.
- Operational risks are proactively managed.
- Governance policies remain current and effective.
- Automated operations remain safe, reliable, and trustworthy.
