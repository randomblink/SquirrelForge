# SquirrelForge Optimization Governance

## Purpose

The Optimization Governance component establishes the policies, standards, approval processes, and oversight required to ensure that all optimization activities within SquirrelForge are safe, measurable, compliant, auditable, and aligned with platform objectives.

Optimization Governance ensures that improvements are based on evidence, validated before implementation, monitored after deployment, and continuously reviewed for effectiveness. It protects platform stability, security, reliability, and maintainability while enabling continuous improvement.

Optimization Governance does not perform optimization activities. It governs how optimizations are proposed, evaluated, approved, implemented, monitored, and retired throughout their lifecycle.

---

# Responsibilities

- Define optimization governance policies.
- Establish optimization standards.
- Approve optimization processes.
- Govern optimization lifecycles.
- Enforce compliance requirements.
- Review optimization effectiveness.
- Manage optimization risks.
- Coordinate governance reviews.
- Record governance activity.
- Continuously improve optimization governance.

# Governance Workflow

1. Receive governance request.
2. Identify applicable policies.
3. Review optimization proposal.
4. Evaluate validation results.
5. Assess operational risks.
6. Approve, defer, or reject optimization.
7. Publish governance decision.
8. Record governance activity.
9. Notify affected components.
10. Schedule post-implementation review.

# Approval Requirements

Optimization approval considers:

- Evidence quality
- Validation status
- Risk level
- Expected benefits
- Rollback readiness
- Resource requirements
- Governance compliance
- Business priorities
- Operational readiness
- Audit completeness

---

# Post-Implementation Review

Governance verifies:

- Expected improvements achieved
- Performance objectives met
- Reliability maintained
- Security unaffected
- Resource utilization improved
- Cost objectives achieved
- Rollback requirements satisfied if needed
- Audit records completed

# Safety Rules

Optimization Governance must never:

- Approve unvalidated optimizations.
- Bypass governance controls.
- Ignore significant operational risks.
- Weaken security requirements.
- Permit undocumented changes.
- Delete protected audit records.
- Override mandatory compliance requirements.

---

# Failure Handling

If governance activities fail:

- Preserve governance evidence.
- Record governance failures.
- Notify the Optimization Manager.
- Escalate unresolved governance issues.
- Maintain existing protections.
- Continue audit recording.
- Schedule governance review.

---

# Audit Requirements

Every governance operation records:

- Governance operation ID
- Timestamp
- Optimization identifier
- Policy evaluated
- Governance decision
- Risk classification
- Reviewer
- Final outcome

---

# Success Criteria

Optimization Governance succeeds when:

- All optimizations follow approved governance processes.
- Evidence and validation support every approval.
- Platform stability, security, and reliability are preserved.
- Optimization outcomes are measurable and auditable.
- Risks are proactively managed.
- Governance policies remain current and effective.
- Continuous improvement occurs without compromising operational integrity.
