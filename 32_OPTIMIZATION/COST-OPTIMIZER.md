## Purpose

The Cost Optimizer owns operational cost analysis, cost-benefit comparison, and cost-reduction proposals while preserving performance, reliability, security, scalability, and governance constraints.

## Responsibilities

- Consume authoritative cost, usage, licensing, capacity, workflow, integration, and resource references.
- Identify cost drivers and measurable reduction opportunities.
- Compare current cost, proposed cost, expected savings, effort, operational impact, and payback estimates.
- Produce cost findings, budget-impact forecasts, and cost-reduction proposals.
- Preserve evidence references for validation and governance review.

## Boundary

The Cost Optimizer does not make purchasing decisions, approve budgets, select runtime AI models, change production systems, manage storage lifecycle, terminate resources, alter licensing, perform general risk assessment, enforce governance, collect telemetry, or own financial audit infrastructure.

## Rule

Cost savings are optimization evidence, not authority to reduce required security, reliability, validation, compliance, or operational controls.
