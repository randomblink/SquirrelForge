# SquirrelForge Cost Optimizer

## Purpose

The Cost Optimizer analyzes operational expenses across SquirrelForge and identifies opportunities to reduce costs while preserving performance, reliability, security, scalability, and governance. It evaluates infrastructure utilization, AI model consumption, storage, networking, licensing, integrations, and workflow execution to recommend cost-effective improvements.

The Cost Optimizer does not directly change production systems or purchasing decisions. It develops evidence-based recommendations and coordinates approved cost optimization activities through the Optimization Manager and governance controls.

---

# Responsibilities

- Analyze operational costs.
- Identify unnecessary expenditures.
- Evaluate infrastructure efficiency.
- Optimize AI model utilization.
- Improve licensing efficiency.
- Recommend cost-saving opportunities.
- Balance cost against performance.
- Support budgeting and planning.
- Record optimization activity.
- Enforce optimization governance.

# Cost Optimization Workflow

1. Receive cost optimization request.
2. Collect operational cost data.
3. Analyze spending patterns.
4. Identify optimization opportunities.
5. Evaluate operational impact.
6. Develop cost-saving recommendations.
7. Submit recommendations for validation.
8. Record audit information.
9. Notify dependent services.
10. Publish optimization status.

# Data Protection

The Cost Optimizer must:

- Protect financial information.
- Protect licensing data.
- Exclude confidential vendor information.
- Enforce governance policies.
- Maintain audit integrity.

---

# Safety Rules

The Cost Optimizer must never:

- Reduce security to save money.
- Compromise platform reliability.
- Recommend unsafe cost reductions.
- Ignore governance requirements.
- Expose confidential financial information.
- Fabricate projected savings.
- Delete financial audit records.

---

# Failure Handling

If cost optimization fails:

- Preserve financial data.
- Record optimization failures.
- Notify the Optimization Manager.
- Retry analysis when appropriate.
- Escalate persistent failures.
- Support contingency planning.
- Maintain audit continuity.

---

# Audit Requirements

Every cost optimization operation records:

- Cost optimization ID
- Timestamp
- Cost category
- Baseline cost
- Projected savings
- Validation status
- Governance status
- Final outcome

---

# Success Criteria

The Cost Optimizer succeeds when:

- Cost-saving opportunities are accurately identified.
- Savings are measurable and sustainable.
- Performance and reliability remain uncompromised.
- Security is never weakened.
- Governance requirements are consistently enforced.
- Budget planning improves.
- Audit records remain complete.
