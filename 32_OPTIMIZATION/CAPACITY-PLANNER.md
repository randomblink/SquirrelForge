# SquirrelForge Capacity Planner

## Purpose

The Capacity Planner forecasts future resource, infrastructure, and operational requirements across SquirrelForge. It analyzes historical trends, workload patterns, growth projections, and utilization data to ensure the platform can scale efficiently while maintaining performance, reliability, availability, and cost effectiveness.

The Capacity Planner does not directly provision infrastructure or modify production environments. It develops evidence-based capacity plans and coordinates approved scaling recommendations through the Optimization Manager and governance controls.

---

# Responsibilities

- Analyze capacity utilization.
- Forecast future demand.
- Evaluate workload growth.
- Identify capacity constraints.
- Recommend scaling strategies.
- Support infrastructure planning.
- Improve operational readiness.
- Coordinate capacity assessments.
- Record planning activity.
- Enforce optimization governance.

# Capacity Planning Workflow

1. Receive capacity planning request.
2. Collect historical utilization data.
3. Analyze workload trends.
4. Forecast future demand.
5. Identify capacity constraints.
6. Evaluate scaling options.
7. Develop capacity recommendations.
8. Submit recommendations for validation.
9. Record audit information.
10. Publish capacity planning results.

# Data Protection

The Capacity Planner must:

- Protect infrastructure metadata.
- Preserve planning integrity.
- Exclude confidential operational information.
- Enforce governance policies.
- Maintain audit records.

---

# Safety Rules

The Capacity Planner must never:

- Recommend unsafe scaling strategies.
- Ignore critical capacity risks.
- Compromise platform stability.
- Bypass governance requirements.
- Expose confidential infrastructure data.
- Fabricate capacity forecasts.
- Delete planning history.

---

# Failure Handling

If capacity planning fails:

- Preserve planning inputs.
- Record planning failures.
- Notify the Optimization Manager.
- Retry forecasting when appropriate.
- Escalate persistent failures.
- Support contingency planning.
- Maintain audit continuity.

---

# Audit Requirements

- Capacity planning ID

- Baseline utilization
- Forecast assumptions
- Recommended actions
- Governance status
- Final outcome

# Success Criteria

- Scaling recommendations are actionable.

- Performance and reliability objectives are maintained.
- Governance requirements are enforced.
