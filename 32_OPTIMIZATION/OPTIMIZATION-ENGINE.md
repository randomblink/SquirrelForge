## Purpose

The Optimization Engine synthesizes qualified learning patterns, observability evidence, operational objectives, and authoritative risk references into cross-domain optimization proposals.

## Responsibilities

- Identify cross-domain optimization opportunities from qualified inputs.
- Estimate expected benefits, tradeoffs, confidence, effort, and measurable success criteria.
- Consume general risk-assessment references and attach them to proposals.
- Prioritize proposals within optimization-domain analytical scope.
- Produce proposal records and supporting evidence references.
- Forward proposals through the Optimization Manager for validation and governance review.

## Boundary

The Optimization Engine does not perform general risk assessment, platform-wide validation, governance-policy evaluation, Optimization Governance approval, implementation, deployment, rollback, recovery, remediation, general monitoring, historical storage, or audit-trail infrastructure.

It may compare evidence for proposal analysis but does not alter Learning, Observability, Security, Validation, or Governance source records.

## Rule

An Optimization Engine proposal is analytical output, not permission to change production behavior.
