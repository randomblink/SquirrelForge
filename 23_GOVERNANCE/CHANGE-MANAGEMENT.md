# SquirrelForge Change Management

Version: 1.0.0
Status: Stable
Owner: Governance Maintainers
Depends On: Versioning, Quality Gates
Used By: Contributors
Last Updated: 2026-07-01

Every material change records motivation, scope, owner, affected dependencies and users, compatibility impact, migration plan, tests, rollout, and rollback. Approval must come from owners of affected stable contracts.
